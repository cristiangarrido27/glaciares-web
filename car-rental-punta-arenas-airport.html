Car rental at Punta Arenas Airport

Land in Punta Arenas and drive straight out of the airport. Glaciares Rent a Car is a local car hire company based in Punta Arenas: we deliver your car or SUV at Presidente Carlos Ibáñez del Campo Airport (PUQ), timed to your flight, and you can return it there at the end of your trip through Patagonia.

How airport delivery works

  • Get a quote and book online or on WhatsApp, with your flight number and arrival time.
  • We meet you at the airport with the vehicle ready, inspected and with a full tank.
  • Sign the rental agreement on the spot and start your trip to Punta Arenas, Puerto Natales or Torres del Paine.
  • Return the car at the airport before your flight home, or at our office in Punta Arenas.

Frequently asked questions

Where do I pick up the car at Punta Arenas Airport?

At Presidente Carlos Ibáñez del Campo International Airport (PUQ). We agree on the exact meeting point and time with you on WhatsApp, based on your flight.

What if my flight is delayed?

Just message us on WhatsApp and we adjust the handover time. That is why we ask for your flight number when you book.

How much does airport delivery cost?

CLP 20,000 for one leg (pick-up only or drop-off only) and CLP 30,000 for both pick-up and drop-off at the airport.

Can I drive to Torres del Paine or Argentina?

Yes. You can drive anywhere in the Magallanes Region, including Torres del Paine, and cross into Argentina with the required permit (CLP 120,000), requested in advance.

Can I rent a car in Chile with a foreign driver's license?

Yes. We accept valid foreign driver's licenses together with your passport.
